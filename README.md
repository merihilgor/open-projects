# Open Projects: Public-Policy and Product Ideas

> Open, bilingual (English / Türkçe) project ideas for researchers, policymakers,
> civic-tech builders and entrepreneurs, free to study and build on for non-commercial use.

**Open Projects** is a collection of detailed project proposals on real-world problems:
**plastic waste and refill stations, earthquake-safe building inspection, wildfire
prevention, child online safety and age verification, road safety, healthy and
damp-free housing, fair payroll timing, innovation marketplaces and accessible voice
control.** Each proposal explains **what** the idea is and **why** it matters: the
problem, the proposed solution, how it works, phasing, stakeholders and risks.

**Türkçe:** Bu depo; plastik atık, deprem güvenliği ve bina muayenesi, orman yangını
önleme, çocukların çevrimiçi güvenliği, trafik güvenliği, sağlıklı konut ve daha
birçok konuda ayrıntılı, iki dilli açık proje fikirleri içerir. Her projenin Türkçe
metni `README.tr.md` dosyasındadır.

## For researchers and AI assistants

- [`llms.txt`](llms.txt): an LLM-friendly index of every project with direct links (see [llmstxt.org](https://llmstxt.org)).
- [`projects.json`](projects.json): a machine-readable catalog with titles (EN/TR), summaries, themes and keywords.
- [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff): citation metadata. Use GitHub's "Cite this repository" button.
- Every project folder is self-contained: `README.md` (English), `README.tr.md` (Turkish), visuals in `assets/`, `LICENSE` and `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`.

Questions, critiques and improvements are welcome through GitHub Issues and Discussions.
